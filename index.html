About Ágora Meats

The Executive Node of International Meat Brokerage

Who We Are

Ágora Meats, LLC is a specialized North American food brokerage and trade facilitation firm headquartered in Miami, Florida—the commercial gateway to the Americas.

Inspired by the ancient Greek Agora, the historic center of market exchange and governance, our firm serves as the central node where commercial demand meets verified U.S. supply. We do not engage in local retail or physical warehousing; instead, we operate as an elite B2B intermediary dedicated to structuring, auditing, and executing high-volume wholesale meat transactions.

Our Mission

To provide international importers and U.S. trading entities with absolute commercial security, transparent deal structures, and faultless documentation auditing—ensuring every container reaches port without operational or regulatory delays.

Services & Capabilities

Strategic Trade Facilitation & Commercial Security

At Ágora Meats, LLC, we streamline the complexities of global meat trade through specialized brokerage and transaction management.

  1. 1.

    Commercial Brokerage & Market Placement

    • We connect qualified international importers with reliable U.S. traders and processors.
    • We negotiate terms, volume commitments, and competitive pricing for bulk beef, pork, and poultry contracts.
  2. 2.

    Documentation Quality Audit & Compliance

    • Pre-Clearance Verification: We audit all export documentation—including USDA/FSIS sanitary certificates, Bills of Lading (BL), and commercial invoices—before departure to prevent customs holds at destination ports.
    • Country-Specific Alignment: We verify that origin plants and products comply with the specific sanitary protocols of the importing country.
  3. 3.

    Financial Structuring & Transaction Facilitation

    • We facilitate secure payment mechanisms, including Letters of Credit (LC) and credit-insured terms (Coface / Atradius coordination).
    • We ensure transparent fee structures and risk-mitigated deal closure for both buyer and supplier.
