01Introduction

Ágora Meats, LLC (“Ágora Meats,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal and business information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect information when you visit our website, submit a business inquiry, apply for or participate in a commercial credit evaluation, communicate with us, or use our meat brokerage and commercial facilitation services.

Ágora Meats operates primarily on a business-to-business (B2B) basis, connecting buyers with suppliers and coordinating commercial transactions and related logistics. Although many of the records we handle relate to businesses, we may also process personal information concerning business owners, authorized representatives, guarantors, and other individuals involved in our operations.

This Policy applies to information handled through our website, business communications, and commercial activities. It should be read together with any applicable contractual terms, credit application documents, authorizations, or other privacy notices we may provide.

02Information We Collect

Depending on the nature of our relationship and the services requested, we may collect the following categories of information:

Business and Contact Information

Company names, business addresses, telephone numbers, business email addresses, and the names and contact details of owners, authorized representatives, buyers, suppliers, and other business contacts.

Business Verification and Legal Documentation

Business registration records, tax identification numbers, business licenses, ownership or authorization documents, and other information reasonably necessary to verify a business or confirm an individual’s authority to act on its behalf.

Financial and Credit Information

Business financial statements, trade references, payment history, credit applications, banking information, and documents used to assess commercial risk or support credit-related arrangements.

Where applicable and lawfully obtained, we may also process personal financial information or documentation relating to an individual who applies for credit, provides a personal guarantee, or participates in a credit evaluation.

Transaction and Logistics Information

Commercial quotations, purchase orders, brokerage agreements, invoices, shipping instructions, bills of lading, consignee details, delivery information, and other records needed to coordinate commercial transactions and shipments.

Website and Communications Information

Information submitted through our website forms, email, telephone, or other communications. Depending on the website tools we use, we may also collect technical information such as IP addresses, browser or device information, and website usage data.

We seek to collect information that is relevant and reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through general website contact forms unless specifically requested through an appropriate process.

03How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect, as applicable, to:

  • Respond to inquiries and prepare commercial quotations, brokerage proposals, and agreements.
  • Introduce and coordinate buyers, suppliers, processors, traders, and other parties involved in proposed or completed transactions.
  • Verify business identities, registration information, authorized representatives, and transaction details.
  • Review commercial credit applications, trade references, payment history, and other information relevant to commercial risk assessments.
  • Coordinate credit assessments, credit insurance applications, or financing arrangements through relevant third parties, where applicable.
  • Arrange shipping, freight forwarding, transportation documentation, and customs-related services through appropriate service providers.
  • Administer invoices, payments, accounting records, tax reporting, and other business documentation.
  • Detect and help prevent fraud, unauthorized activity, and misuse of our services.
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, recordkeeping, and lawful information-request requirements.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website and respond to communications submitted through its forms.
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims and protect our legitimate business interests, where permitted by law.

We process personal information for purposes consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

04How We Share Your Information

Ágora Meats does not sell personal information for monetary consideration. We may disclose relevant business or personal information when reasonably necessary to conduct our operations, fulfill a transaction, provide requested services, or comply with applicable law.

Information may be shared with the following categories of recipients:

Buyers, Suppliers, and Commercial Partners

We may share relevant business and transaction information with buyers, suppliers, meat processors, traders, and other commercial parties involved in evaluating or fulfilling a transaction.

Credit and Financial Service Providers

We may disclose relevant information to banks, commercial credit information providers, credit insurers, and other financial service providers involved in a credit evaluation or financial arrangement.

Shipping and Logistics Providers

We may share relevant information with freight forwarders, shipping lines, port service providers, customs brokers, and other parties responsible for transportation, shipping documentation, or cargo clearance.

Professional and Technology Service Providers

We may disclose information to legal and accounting professionals, website hosting providers, email and business software providers, document storage services, and other vendors supporting our business operations.

Governmental and Regulatory Authorities

We may disclose information to tax agencies, customs authorities, courts, law enforcement agencies, or other governmental authorities when required by law or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Other Authorized or Legally Permitted Recipients

We may disclose information with appropriate authorization or when otherwise permitted by law, including when reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We seek to limit disclosures to information relevant to the applicable purpose. Where appropriate, service providers processing personal information on our behalf may be subject to contractual confidentiality, security, and data protection obligations.

Information disclosed to commercial counterparties may be subject to their own privacy policies and legal obligations. We do not control the independent privacy practices of third parties acting on their own behalf.

05International Data Transfers

Our business may involve buyers, suppliers, logistics providers, financial institutions, and other commercial partners located in different countries.

As a result, information may be transferred to or accessed in the United States and other countries where parties involved in a transaction or our service providers operate.

Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to handle international transfers in accordance with relevant legal requirements and to protect information appropriately.

06Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, maintain security, understand website usage, and improve the visitor experience.

The information collected through these technologies depends on the tools and settings used on our website. Where applicable, we will provide additional information or choices concerning cookies and similar technologies as required by law.

Visitors may also be able to manage cookies through their browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

07Data Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal and business information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

Depending on the nature of the information and the risks involved, these safeguards may include access restrictions, appropriate authentication measures, secure document storage and transmission, confidentiality requirements, and procedures for managing information security incidents.

Access to sensitive financial and credit documentation is limited to authorized individuals and service providers who require the information for legitimate business purposes.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Although we take reasonable steps to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We maintain appropriate procedures for assessing and responding to suspected security incidents and providing notifications when required by applicable law.

08Data Retention

We retain personal and business information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, maintain commercial and credit records, satisfy applicable legal and tax requirements, administer agreements, and resolve disputes.

Retention periods depend on the type of information, the nature of the transaction, applicable recordkeeping requirements, and whether a legal claim, investigation, or other obligation requires continued retention.

When information is no longer reasonably required and no legal obligation or other valid basis requires its retention, we take reasonable steps to delete it or securely dispose of it.

Information maintained in routine backups may remain for a limited period until those backups are securely overwritten or deleted, subject to applicable requirements.

09Privacy Rights and Requests

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of the information we maintain, individuals may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, as well as other rights provided by law.

These rights may be subject to legal exceptions, including obligations to retain commercial, accounting, tax, credit, or other records; comply with lawful requests; establish or defend legal claims; or fulfill contractual obligations.

To submit a privacy request, please contact us using the information in the Contact Us section.

We may request information reasonably necessary to verify your identity and, where applicable, your authority to act on behalf of another individual or business.

We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law. Where required, we will explain if a request cannot be fulfilled in whole or in part.

This Policy does not create rights beyond those provided by applicable law.

10Third-Party Websites and Services

Our website or communications may contain links to third-party websites or services. Those websites and services are governed by their own privacy policies and practices.

Ágora Meats is not responsible for the privacy, security, or content practices of third-party websites that we do not operate or control. We encourage visitors to review the applicable privacy policies before submitting information to third parties.

11Children’s Privacy

Our website and commercial services are intended for business use and are not directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through our website for purposes unrelated to our legitimate business operations. If we become aware that information has been collected inappropriately, we will take reasonable steps to address the matter in accordance with applicable law.

12Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business operations, information practices, technology, or applicable legal requirements.

The updated Policy will be posted on our website with a revised “Last Updated” date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before implementing material changes.

We encourage visitors and business partners to review this Policy periodically.
