01Who We Are

Ágora Meats, LLC (“Ágora Meats,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a B2B meat brokerage and trade facilitation firm headquartered in Miami, Florida, USA.

This website is intended for business use by importers, distributors, suppliers, processors, and traders. It is not directed to consumers or to individuals under 18 years of age.

02Information We Collect

Information you provide to us. When you submit the Trade Inquiry form or contact us, we may collect:

  • Contact details: full name, company name, corporate email, and phone / WhatsApp number.
  • Business profile: your role (importer / buyer, supplier / processor / trader, or distributor) and destination country.
  • Trade specifications: protein category, cut classification, required volume, target shipment window, delivery terms, and any notes or certification requirements you include.
  • Correspondence: the content of emails, calls, or messages you exchange with our team.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and the date and time of your visit. If we use cookies or analytics tools, we will describe them in this policy and, where required, request your consent.

03How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and prepare quotations.
  • Connect qualified importers with U.S. suppliers, processors, and traders, and negotiate and structure transactions.
  • Audit and verify export documentation, sanitary certifications, and country-specific requirements.
  • Coordinate secure payment mechanisms, such as Letters of Credit and credit-insured terms.
  • Communicate with you about your inquiry or ongoing transactions.
  • Maintain business records and comply with legal, regulatory, tax, and export obligations.
  • Protect the security and integrity of our website and business.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for consumer advertising.

04How We Share Information

We share information only as needed to carry out the purposes above, including with:

  • Trade counterparties: U.S. suppliers, processors, traders, and international buyers involved in a transaction you have asked us to facilitate.
  • Financial and credit institutions: banks issuing or advising Letters of Credit and credit insurers (such as Coface or Atradius) when structuring payment terms.
  • Service providers: companies that host our website, provide email, or support our operations, under obligations to protect the information.
  • Authorities: government agencies, such as USDA/FSIS or customs authorities, when required for export documentation or by law.
  • Professional advisors: attorneys, accountants, and auditors bound by confidentiality.

05International Transfers

Ágora Meats is based in the United States. If you contact us from outside the United States, your information will be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States, and may be shared with counterparties in other countries involved in your transaction. Data protection laws in these countries may differ from those in your country.

06Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry, carry out transactions, and meet our legal, accounting, and record-keeping obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

07Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to ask us to stop sending you communications. Depending on your country of residence, you may have additional rights under local data protection laws. We will respond to requests as required by applicable law.

09Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites, and we encourage you to review their policies.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
